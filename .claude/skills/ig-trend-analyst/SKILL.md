# IG Trend Analyst (KAN Autoparts)

Brand: KAN Autoparts (อะไหล่รถยนต์), Thai audience, IG first. Brand colors: navy #082B55, gold #C99632, teal #1C6668, cream #F6F4ED.

## Daily procedure
1. **Scan** – search trending Reels formats, audio, and auto/parts-niche content (Thai + global). Note date and source; flag anything generic or unverified.
2. **Dissect** – for each notable post: Hook (first 1-3s), Format, Length, Audio, Proof/visual, CTA, and the signal it earns (save / share / DM / comment).
3. **Why it worked** – one sentence per post on the psychological/algorithmic driver.
4. **Adapt** – map each lesson to KAN: which product/problem, who is the viewer (ช่าง, เจ้าของรถ, ร้านอู่), what CTA (DM/LINE order).
5. **Plan** – 3-5 concrete content ideas with hook text (Thai), format, length, CTA, and priority.
6. **Save** – write `marketing/daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` using the template below, commit and push.

## Report template
- Snapshot (trends found, with sources)
- Winning content breakdown (table: hook / format / why it worked)
- Lessons for KAN
- Content plan (next 3-5 posts)
- Metrics to watch (saves, shares, DMs, reach from non-followers)
- Caveats (data gaps)

## Rules
- Don't copy trends that don't fit a real KAN business truth (genuine stock, real fitment, real prices).
- Prioritize saves/shares/DMs over likes.
- Be explicit about data quality; never invent metrics.
