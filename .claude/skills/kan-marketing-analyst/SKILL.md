# KAN Marketing Analyst (daily, IG first)

1. Search trends: Thai IG Reels/Carousel trends, auto-parts/garage/car-care content, trending audio/hashtags. Search in Thai and English.
2. Pick 3-5 high-performing examples. For each: format, hook (first 3s), topic, CTA, why it worked (emotion, utility, proof, relatability).
3. Mark confidence: verified (seen source) vs. directional (vendor stats). Never invent view counts.
4. Translate into KAN: product (อะไหล่), audience (ช่าง/ร้านอู่/เจ้าของรถ), brand tone (navy/gold, trustworthy).
5. Output `reports/daily-trend/YYYY-MM-DD.md`: Trends, Why it works, 3 content ideas (format/hook/caption/CTA), what to test, KPIs.
6. Keep running log of tested ideas to avoid repeats.
