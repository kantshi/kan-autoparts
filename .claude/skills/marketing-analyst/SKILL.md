# Marketing Analyst — KAN Autoparts

Daily routine for a marketer reviewing market and content trends, understanding
why specific content succeeds, and translating that into a content plan for
KAN Autoparts (ร้านอะไหล่รถยนต์). **Instagram is the priority channel** —
analyze and plan for IG first before other platforms.

## Brand context (read before planning)

- Business: online/offline auto parts retailer, order management via a
  product-catalog storefront (`index.html` at repo root — Thai-language UI).
- Visual identity: navy (`#082B55`), gold/champagne (`#C99632`, `#D4A447`),
  teal (`#1C6668`, `#0C4144`), cream background, Sarabun font.
- Audience: Thai car owners and DIY/semi-DIY buyers looking up parts by SKU,
  comparing prices, and wanting confidence they're buying the right part.

## Daily workflow

1. **Scan trends** — search for current IG/short-form content trends (this
   week/month), plus auto-parts / auto-repair specific content ideas. Cover
   both global trend reports and Thai-market sources when available.
2. **Analyze successful examples** — for each notable trend or example found,
   write down *why* it works: hook type, format (Reels/carousel/single),
   emotional driver, pacing, what makes it save/share-worthy. Don't just list
   trends — explain the mechanism behind the success.
3. **Adapt to KAN** — translate each success factor into a specific,
   shootable content idea for KAN: a hook line, format, and the auto-parts
   angle (installation tips, SKU/compatibility mistakes, before/after,
   customer proof, behind-the-scenes at the shop/warehouse).
4. **Prioritize IG** — output an IG-first content plan (Reels > carousel >
   single post, in that order, unless evidence says otherwise this cycle).
   Note format, hook, cadence, and the one metric each piece should move
   (saves/shares/comments/DMs).
5. **Save the output** — write the day's analysis + plan to
   `marketing/content-plans/YYYY-MM-DD-ig-trend-analysis.md` using the
   sections: Trend Summary, Why It Works, Applied Ideas for KAN, This Week's
   IG Plan, Next Steps. Keep prior days' files — they build a trend history.
6. **Flag, don't fabricate** — this skill has no direct connection to KAN's
   real Instagram Insights/analytics. Base "why it succeeded" analysis on
   public trend research and general engagement mechanics, and say so
   explicitly. If/when real IG performance data (exports, screenshots, a
   connected analytics source) is available, prefer it over public trend
   research and note which source each conclusion came from.

## Output quality bar

- Every trend cited needs a one-line "why it works" and a KAN-specific
  adaptation — a trend list with no adaptation is not a finished analysis.
- Content ideas must be concrete enough to shoot tomorrow (hook line +
  shot list gist), not generic advice like "post more videos."
- Keep it short: a daily analyst report, not a strategy deck. Aim for one
  page per day.
