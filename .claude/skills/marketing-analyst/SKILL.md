# Marketing Analyst — KAN Autoparts

Daily routine for a KAN marketer to study what's working in the market right
now and turn it into a concrete Instagram content plan for KAN (an auto-parts
ordering/e-commerce business in Thailand — see `index.html` for the product
and brand context: navy/gold/teal palette, Thai-language UI, bulk-price
tiers, SKU-driven catalog).

## Process

1. **Scan trends, not just this account.** Research current short-form
   video / Instagram trends generally, plus anything specific to
   automotive parts, aftermarket accessories, or Thai auto/e-commerce
   content. Use web search — don't rely on stale knowledge, trends move
   weekly.
2. **Find 2-4 concrete examples of content that is succeeding** (a format,
   a hook, a brand, a campaign — ideally in automotive/aftermarket or
   Thai retail, but a strong cross-industry example is fine too).
3. **Diagnose why each example works**, not just what it is. Look for the
   mechanism: hook timing, authenticity vs. production value, whether it
   solves a real problem, whether it's shareable/saveable, whether it
   builds a personal-recommendation feel vs. catalog broadcast.
4. **Translate each mechanism into a KAN-specific IG idea** — a real post
   or Reel concept using KAN's actual product context (parts catalog,
   bulk pricing, SKUs, installation, mechanics/shop-owner customers).
   Prioritize Instagram; note other channels only if the idea travels well.
5. **Write the output** to `marketing/content-plans/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (see
   `marketing/content-plans/TEMPLATE.md` for the structure) with sources
   linked. Keep it short enough to act on same-day: a trend summary and a
   short list of ready-to-shoot content ideas, not a research report.
6. **Look back before planning forward.** Skim the last few files in
   `marketing/content-plans/` so ideas build on what was already proposed
   instead of repeating it.

## What "good" looks like for KAN

- Favor low-production, authentic formats (talking-head, real install
  footage, honest behind-the-scenes) over polished ads — this consistently
  outperforms for trust-driven, considered purchases like auto parts.
- Favor content that solves a real problem or answers a real question
  (e.g. "this noise means this part is failing") over pure product
  showcases — it drives saves, shares, and DMs, which is what the
  algorithm and word-of-mouth both reward.
- Treat the account as a community/advice channel, not a catalog
  broadcast — the single most common mistake aftermarket brands make.
- Every idea should name: the hook (first 3 seconds), the format (Reel /
  carousel / post), and the specific KAN product or catalog angle it uses.
