## Trends
- **Engagement signals:** Oct 2026 guidance says IG rewards DM shares, saves, replies, business conversations over likes. Reels = discovery, carousels = trust. ([MEAN CEO](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=10941))
- **Search-friendly posts:** one buyer phrase per post, repeated in caption, on-screen text, and voice. For KAN: model + part + part number.
- **Audio:** disco revival ("Ring My Bell"), "Timber", Sabrina Carpenter "Go Go Juice"; process-style original sounds work for business accounts. ([SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-trends?format=md))
- **Lifespan:** audio trends peak 7–10 days; format trends 3–4 weeks → prefer formats. ([PostNitro](https://postnitro.ai/blog/post/instagram-trends))
- **Format:** "Netflix documentary sit-down" talking format ([Virlo](https://virlo.ai/blog/whats-trending-on-instagram)).

## Why these work (analysis)
Save/share-worthy utility + clear hook + a reason to DM. Parts content is naturally utility content.

## Content ideas for KAN
1. **Reel – "Wrong part vs right part"** (format: quick compare). Hook: "ซื้ออะไหล่ผิดรุ่น เสียเงินฟรี". Show old vs new part, on-screen model + part number. CTA: DM รุ่นรถ รับเช็คอะไหล่ให้.
2. **Carousel – "5 อาการที่ต้องเปลี่ยน [part]"** (trust/saves). Last slide CTA + LINE/DM.
3. **Reel – Packing process (ASMR, process audio)**: orders packed, part number shown. Builds trust, fits business-friendly audio.
Optional: sit-down "ช่างเล่าให้ฟัง" Reel on common mistakes.
