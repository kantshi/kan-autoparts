# KAN Content Plan — 2026-09-15

## Trend scan (what's working right now)

- **Raw, low-production Reels are beating polished ads.** Talking-head clips,
  honest behind-the-scenes, and single-take videos that show real work and
  real judgment are outperforming glossy posts — because they read as
  trustworthy, not as an ad. Reels now drive ~50% of time spent on
  Instagram and reach 2x+ what a static photo gets.
- **The first 2–3 seconds decide everything.** Winning content is made
  faster, shorter, and front-loaded with the hook — no slow intros.
- **Shares/saves > likes.** Posts win when someone sends them to a
  colleague or friend because the content is specific and immediately
  useful (a fact, a fix, a warning) — not just nice to look at. Saveable
  carousels and "searchable" captions (answering a real question in
  plain language) are a named September 2026 trend.
- **Aftermarket/auto-parts specific:** the brands that win on social treat
  the account as a **community/advice channel**, not a product catalog
  broadcast — car content is inherently visual and tribal, and it travels
  when it looks like a trusted creator's honest install + review rather
  than a sales pitch (see Hoonigan). On the opposite end, wildly
  unpolished, single-purpose content (an auto-parts seller filming
  literally just the part and the fix, nothing else) has gone viral
  precisely because it's unvarnished and un-produced ("Auto Parts Tina").
  Both examples point the same direction for KAN: **less catalog, more
  real work on camera.**

## Applied to KAN — Instagram content ideas

### 1. "เสียงนี้บอกอะไร" (What this noise means) — diagnostic Reel series
- **Format:** Reel (15–25s)
- **Hook (first 3s):** Play the actual noise/symptom first, no talking —
  "เสียงแบบนี้... รถคุณกำลังจะพังตรงไหน?"
- **Content:** Staff diagnoses a real symptom on camera, shows the worn
  part pulled from a customer's car, names the KAN SKU that fixes it.
- **Why it should work:** Solves a real, searchable problem → drives
  saves/shares; single-take and unscripted = the low-production trust
  effect.
- **CTA:** "Comment your car's noise, we'll tell you what it is" — seeds
  the next episode and drives comments (community, not broadcast).

### 2. Real install, not stock photo — "ก่อนติดตั้ง 60 วินาที"
- **Format:** Reel (30–60s)
- **Hook:** Show the broken/worn part close-up first — "อย่าใส่อะไหล่ผิด
  ก่อนเปลี่ยนดูตรงนี้ก่อน"
- **Content:** Real installation of a bestselling SKU from KAN's catalog,
  filmed handheld/phone quality on the shop floor — not a studio shot.
- **Why it should work:** Mirrors the Hoonigan "trusted creator installs +
  reviews" mechanic — reads as a recommendation, not an ad.
- **CTA:** Link/SKU code in caption + "สั่งได้เลย ราคาส่งในแคปชัน".

### 3. OEM vs. เทียบ (aftermarket) — saveable comparison carousel
- **Format:** Carousel (5–6 slides)
- **Hook (slide 1):** "อะไหล่แท้ vs เทียบ ต่างกันตรงไหน ก่อนซื้อต้องรู้"
- **Content:** Plain-language comparison (lifespan, price, when each makes
  sense) using KAN's actual price tiers/bulk pricing as the worked example.
- **Why it should work:** Answers a real pre-purchase question people
  screenshot/save for later — exactly the "searchable, useful" format
  that's over-performing right now.
- **CTA:** "เซฟไว้เทียบก่อนซื้อ" (explicit save prompt).

### 4. Customer car reveal — UGC repost
- **Format:** Reel or Post, resharing a customer's install
- **Hook:** Customer's own before/after or sound clip.
- **Content:** Repost a real buyer's car with the KAN part fitted, tag
  them, short caption on what was replaced and why.
- **Why it should work:** Authentic third-party proof outperforms brand
  self-promotion; costs nothing to produce.
- **CTA:** "ส่งคลิปรถคุณมา มีสิทธิ์ได้ลงเพจ" (call for more UGC).

### 5. "ซื้อเยอะคุ้มแค่ไหน" — bulk pricing explainer
- **Format:** Carousel
- **Hook:** "ร้านซ่อม/อู่ อ่านนี่ก่อนสั่งของเดือนนี้"
- **Content:** Walk through KAN's bulk-price tiers (already in the app UI)
  with real numbers for a popular SKU — targets shop-owner/mechanic
  buyers specifically, not just retail end-customers.
- **Why it should work:** Directly useful to KAN's highest-value segment
  (repeat B2B buyers); positions KAN as the practical choice, not just a
  catalog.
- **CTA:** "ทักแชทเพื่อขอราคาส่ง".

## Notes / carry-forward
- Next run: track which of the 5 formats above actually got shot, and
  pull real engagement numbers before proposing new ones — don't keep
  generating ideas without a feedback loop.
- Avoid pure product-showcase posts (stock photo + price) — this is the
  "catalog broadcast" pattern the research flags as the common losing
  move for aftermarket brands.
