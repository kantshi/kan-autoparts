## 1. Trend Summary

- **Low-production, founder/staff-led clips beat polished ads.** Talking-head
  and behind-the-scenes (BTS) content is outperforming glossy product shots;
  26% of Gen Z specifically want more BTS content from brands in 2026.
- **Shares/saves > likes.** Instagram's algorithm now weighs DM-shares and
  saves above raw views or likes — a post wins when someone sends it to a
  friend because it's specific and immediately useful.
- **The hook has ~1–3 seconds.** 71% of viewers decide to keep watching (or
  not) in the opening seconds; contrarian or myth-busting hooks work because
  the brain can't leave a contradiction unresolved.
- **Education content builds trust for parts retailers specifically.**
  Install/compatibility tutorials, "how to pick the right part" and "common
  buying mistakes" content is the recurring winning pattern across auto-parts
  and auto-repair accounts.
- **UGC and real-shop photos outperform stock imagery.** Genuine, slightly
  imperfect footage of the actual shop/warehouse/customers reads as more
  trustworthy than staged product photography.

## 2. Why It Works (mechanism, not just the trend)

| Pattern | Why it succeeds |
|---|---|
| Talking-head / BTS clips | Signals a real person with real judgment → trust; cheap to produce → can post often, which the algorithm rewards |
| Save/share-driven posts | Instagram explicitly ranks for share velocity; content that is "useful enough to send to someone" gets algorithmic lift no ad spend can buy |
| Contrarian/mistake hooks ("อย่าซื้อ...ถ้ายังไม่รู้เรื่องนี้") | Creates an open loop the viewer must resolve; forces a full watch-through, which drives retention signals |
| Install/compatibility tutorials | Solves a real, specific pre-purchase anxiety ("ใส่รถรุ่นนี้ได้ไหม") → high save rate because viewers keep it for later reference |
| Real, imperfect shop footage | Contrasts with generic stock/competitor content → stands out in feed, reads as authentic proof the shop is real and stocked |

## 3. Applied Ideas for KAN (auto parts, navy/gold/teal brand)

1. **"ซื้ออะไหล่ผิด เสียเงินฟรี" mistake series (Reels, contrarian hook)**
   Hook: "อย่าเพิ่งซื้อผ้าเบรกจนกว่าจะดูคลิปนี้" → 15–20s, staff explains one
   common buying mistake (wrong SKU, non-OEM vs OEM, fake parts) + how KAN
   verifies compatibility. CTA: "เซฟไว้เช็คก่อนซื้อ" (drives saves).

2. **"เช็คก่อนใส่" compatibility mini-tutorials (Reels, education)**
   60–90s, one part category per episode (ไส้กรอง, ผ้าเบรก, แบตเตอรี่). Show
   SKU lookup on the storefront UI → install/checking tips. Save-worthy
   reference content; ties directly to the order-management catalog.

3. **Warehouse/shop BTS (Reels, low-production, staff-led)**
   Quick, unscripted clips: restocking, packing an order, a staff member
   answering "คำถามที่ลูกค้าถามบ่อย". Builds the human trust signal Gen Z
   audiences want; cheap enough to shoot 2–3x/week.

4. **Customer proof carousel (Carousel, UGC)**
   Before/after or "ลูกค้าส่งรูปหลังเปลี่ยนอะไหล่" posts, real photos (not
   stock), short caption with the part + SKU. Doubles as searchable
   product-proof content.

5. **"เลือกยังไงให้ไม่โดนหลอก" myth-busting single post (carousel, hook-first)**
   Contrarian first slide ("อะไหล่ถูกกว่าตลาด 50% ระวัง!") walking through
   red flags when buying auto parts online — positions KAN as the trustworthy
   source, drives shares to friends/family shopping for parts.
