## 1. Trend snapshot
- Instagram now uses **Views** as the primary metric across Reels, Stories, photos and carousels.
- Reels are ~50% of time spent on Instagram; **watch time, likes and sends** are the strongest signals. **Sends (DM shares) weigh roughly 3-5x a like** for reaching non-followers.
- **Carousels** have the highest engagement rate (~0.50% by followers); each swipe counts, and unfinished carousels get re-shown in Feed. Smaller accounts should lean on **Reels for reach**.
- Ideal Reel length cited: **30-90s**; scroll-stop rate and completion rate drive distribution most.
- Instagram added a feature letting users tune their Reels recommendations (Thai tech press, Sanook) - so **clear niche signals** (consistent topic, keywords, captions) matter more.

## 2. Why it works, applied to KAN
| Signal | Why it wins | KAN adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Sends | People forward useful/relatable posts to friends/mechanics | "ส่งให้ช่างของคุณ" content: part compatibility, failure signs |
| Completion | Short payoff-driven story | Before/after, old vs new part, "wrong vs right part" |
| Carousel slide 2 | Retains swipes | Slide 1 hook, slide 2 = surprising fact/price tip |
| Niche consistency | Users now steer recs | Consistent auto-parts topics, Thai keywords in captions |

## 3. Content ideas for KAN
**Reels**
1. "3 อาการที่บอกว่าต้องเปลี่ยน [ชิ้นส่วน]" - hook in 3s, show worn part, end with SKU + LINE OA CTA. Send-worthy for mechanics.
2. Old vs new part side-by-side (unboxing/teardown, 30-45s) with the bulk-price tag as the closer.
3. "อะไหล่แท้ vs เทียบ ดูยังไง" - quick spot-the-difference test; caption asks viewers to send to a friend.

**Carousels**
1. "เช็กลิสต์ก่อนสั่งอะไหล่ ไม่ให้สั่งผิดรุ่น" (7 slides; slide 2 = the common mistake).
2. "ราคาส่ง vs ปลีก" - explain bulk tiers with simple examples; save-worthy for shop owners.
