## Trends found
1. **Before/after & "upside down/inside out" transition formats** (Oct 2026 roundups) - suits install/repair reveals.
2. **Niche content beats broad content** - focused auto-parts account is well positioned.
3. **Reels = reach to non-followers; carousels ~equal engagement** (0.50% vs 0.48% cited) - mix both.
4. **Reels up to 90s** - room for short how-to / part-comparison clips.
5. **AI car-lifestyle Reels** going viral (Sept 2026, single source) - low-budget premium look; use cautiously, KAN's trust comes from real parts.
6. **Timely hook:** Thailand Super Series final at Buriram, 30 Oct - 1 Nov 2026 (car-culture moment).
7. Dealer-style Reels reportedly get 3-5x impressions vs photo-only posts (localshift, vendor claim).

## Why it works (analysis)
- Before/after: instant payoff in 3s, clear utility -> high retention.
- Niche: tells the algorithm and viewer exactly who it's for -> better saves/shares.
- Short tutorials: save-worthy utility, drives DMs.
