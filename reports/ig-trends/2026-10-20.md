# KAN IG Trend Report — 2026-10-20

## Trend snapshot
- **Reels drive sharing (Thailand):** Meta Thailand: 85% of content shared via IG chat is Reels; ~70% of Thai consumers use IG DMs to help purchase decisions; >50% use IG to discover products/brands. *(Meta-reported figures — treat with caution.)* — [Thairath](https://en.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/tech/2940434), [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/instagram-reels-thailand-campaign/)
- **Algorithm signals 2026:** watch-through, saves, shares and follows outweigh likes; hook within 3 seconds; 30–90s often performs best (Buffer, via secondary blogs). — [inro.social](https://www.inro.social/blog/how-instagram-reels-work-2025), [Postiz](https://postiz.com/blog/instagram-viral-triggers-2026)
- **Trial Reels:** test clips with non-followers; IG is rate-limiting abusive use, so vary the real angle, not just text. — [Postiz](https://postiz.com/blog/instagram-viral-triggers-2026)
- **Formats suited to mechanic niche:** problem→fix, myth-busting, behind-the-scenes of real work; authenticity > polish. 

## Why it works
Utility + shareability: a clip that solves a frustrating problem gets saved and DM'd to a friend/mechanic ("ส่งให้ช่างดู"), which the algorithm rewards and which feeds the DM-to-buy behavior.

## Apply to KAN (3 ideas)
1. **"อาการนี้ = อะไหล่ตัวนี้"** (30–45s): hook "รถมีเสียงนี้ อย่าเพิ่งเปลี่ยนทั้งชุด" → show symptom, part, correct fit. CTA: "ส่งรุ่นรถมาทาง DM เช็คอะไหล่ให้ฟรี".
2. **Myth-bust:** "อะไหล่แท้ vs OEM ต่างกันยังไง" with side-by-side shots; save-worthy checklist on last frame.
3. **Behind-the-scenes order packing:** real order → QC check → ship; builds trust (cream/navy/gold branding, no heavy production).

## Test plan
Post idea 1 as Trial Reel + normal Reel with a different hook; compare watch-through, saves, shares, DM count after 48h.
